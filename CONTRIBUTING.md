# Contributing

Contributions should improve factual accuracy, accessibility, documentation, or validation without weakening rights and privacy boundaries.

- Do not add source images, scene assets, archives, prompts, receipts, logs, credentials, contact addresses, account identifiers, or local filesystem paths.
- Do not present fan-fictional descriptions as biography.
- Preserve pending, provisional, documentation-only, and accepted-omission labels.
- Submit full-resolution artwork through a private maintainer review path, not through Git.
- Run `python3 scripts/verify_repository.py --root .` before proposing a change.

A change is not release-ready until JSON, links, image decoding, metadata, checksums, counts, and pending-state validation all pass.
