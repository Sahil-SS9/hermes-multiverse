## Documented record

The current library contains source portraits and character sheets for Brooklyn / Heartbreaker, together with the complete nine-style set.

The legacy NRCU record identifies Brooklyn in the fan-made video [*Thank You Nous Research*](../music-videos/thank-you-nous-research-arts-bro.md) at about **00:40–00:42**, painting a Nous poster or logo panel in a workshop-like vehicle space. That appearance establishes the visual cameo, not a new biography or official role.

Brooklyn is also named in the archived descriptions of [The Campfire](../scenes/campfire.md), [The Carnival Party](../scenes/carnival.md), and [The Beach Finale](../scenes/beach-finale.md). Those scene pages preserve NRCU descriptions; their original scene images are unavailable.

## NRCU character lore

The legacy sheet presents Heartbreaker as a 5'7" lean, athletic "Open-Source Rebel" and builder whose signature item is a pink laptop. It depicts voluminous curly brown hair, a pink heart-patterned dress, pink heels, and heart-and-skull laptop stickers. Its language about fighting closed systems with charm, code, and conviction is fan-created character lore. It is not a verified personal profile, employment record, or statement of official Nous Research canon.
