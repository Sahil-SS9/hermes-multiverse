## Content-rights issue route

Open a repository issue titled **Content-rights issue** and identify the affected public path. Do not post private evidence or credentials. A maintainer should immediately make the repository private, remove the affected release asset, rebuild from corrected private source, purge history if sensitive material entered Git, and verify a fresh clone before restoring visibility.

Changing visibility cannot recall copies or forks already made by third parties.
