## Catalogue design

Sidbin's established visual record depicts a tall, slim figure with shaggy dark hair, a long dark coat, round mirrored lenses, scuffed boots, an eye-sigil book, and purple or teal smoke-like energy. The catalogue has nine current style sheets but no asset in Sidbin's `source` category; the anime sheet shown above is therefore a style reference, not an original source sheet.

## NRCU character lore

The legacy character sheet calls Sidbin a wandering observer, scout, and analyst whose allegiance is unknown. It assigns no conventional weapon and instead uses secrets, a reflective intel shard, mirrored lenses, and the eye-sigil book as narrative devices. The profile's language about hidden truths, fractured motives, and "smoke and mirrors" is fan-created story material. It is not a factual profile of a person or proof of official Nous Research canon.

The archived descriptions place Sidbin in [The Campfire](../scenes/campfire.md), [The Carnival Party](../scenes/carnival.md), and [The Beach Finale](../scenes/beach-finale.md). Those pages preserve NRCU scene descriptions; the exact source images are unavailable.
