## Catalogue design

Teknium's established visual record uses spiky green hair with leaf-like detail, one connected sheet of triangular shades, a dark-green tactical-style jacket with `NR` shoulder patches, a winged-H command staff, and a glowing tablet. The current design specifies shades rather than a mask, visor, or glowing eyes.

## NRCU character lore

The legacy sheet casts Teknium as the Hermes Agent Squad's leader and architect, focused on systems, strategy, and growth. The command staff and tablet carry that fictional role. These labels describe fan-created character material, not a verified real-world title, employment record, or official Nous Research canon.

The NRCU video log places Teknium at about **01:50** in [*Thank You Nous Research*](../music-videos/thank-you-nous-research-arts-bro.md), in a tower-like operations room. The archive attributes the scene to helping people worldwide, but that interpretation is retained as source commentary.

Teknium also appears in the archived scene descriptions for [ASK HERMES](../scenes/ask-hermes.md), [The Campfire](../scenes/campfire.md), [The Carnival Party](../scenes/carnival.md), and [The Beach Finale](../scenes/beach-finale.md). ASK HERMES records a conflicting red face covering from an unavailable legacy image; the discrepancy remains unresolved. The [Tekpunk production record](../music-videos/tekpunk-one-more-prompt.md) contains a creator-supplied "leaked from Teknium's hard disk" joke, not a confirmed appearance.
