# The Carnival Party

> **Catalogue status:** WIP scene record derived from the 123mikeyd NRCU Vault.

## Recorded scene

The NRCU description frames the scene as a neon-lit tech hackathon party thrown by Nous Research to celebrate **5000+ PRs**. The crowd, coding stations and carnival attractions are presented as deliberate chaos.

### Characters recorded in the scene

- [Turbo Fit](../characters/turbo-fit.md), in the left foreground, plays guitar after a clothed monkey has thrown a cream pie into his face. The source calls him dazed but energetic.
- [Brooklyn / Heartbreaker](../characters/brooklyn-heartbreaker.md), in the centre, smiles while holding a tray of treats and cupcakes.
- [Teknium](../characters/teknium.md), in the centre-right background, points forward with a mischievous, confident grin and holds a tablet.
- [Tinuviel](../characters/tinuviel.md), right of centre, works at a computer displaying code; her star-patterned dress is visible.
- [Sidbin](../characters/sidbin.md), at far right, wears sunglasses, holds a book or tablet and looks to the side.

No other named character appearance is established by the source page.

## Monkey crowd

The legacy record describes dozens of clothed monkeys filling the carnival:

- a monkey in a striped shirt throws the pie at Turbo Fit
- a monkey in a green hoodie eats a banana at a monitor
- a monkey in a purple hoodie eats popcorn
- a monkey wearing headphones codes at a laptop
- a monkey plays drums
- several monkeys work at computer stations

The source ends this section with the joke that the monkeys are the development team. It does not identify them as named catalogue characters.

## Recorded details

- a **"5000+ PRs"** sign
- **"NR NOUS RESEARCH"** branding on clothing and devices
- a large illuminated **H** logo
- a Ferris wheel, string lights and carnival-game booths
- screens showing code and interface elements

## Recorded style and mood

The source describes highly detailed, textured comic art lit by neon and strings of lights against a dark carnival sky. Its visual mix joins pop culture, coding culture and a party setting.

The recorded mood is celebratory, joyful and deliberately ridiculous.
