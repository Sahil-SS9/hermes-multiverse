## Catalogue design

Tinuviel's source sheet depicts a slender figure with long dark hair, a dark-blue gown covered in gold celestial patterns, bare feet, and cosmic or floral motifs. A celestial astrolabe, ship's wheel, nebula, and golden flower recur as visual symbols.

## NRCU character lore

The legacy sheet calls Tinuviel "The Starry Wanderer" and casts her as a guardian, guide, and source of influence rather than a conventional fighter. It assigns an Open Source Alliance allegiance and frames knowledge, wisdom, and inspiration as her focus. These details are fan-created character lore. They are not a factual biography, an official affiliation, or confirmed Nous Research canon.

The appearance log for [*Thank You Nous Research*](../music-videos/thank-you-nous-research-arts-bro.md) places Tinuviel at about **00:25–00:28**, kneeling among rocks and foliage in the star-patterned gown.

The archived descriptions also place her in [The Campfire](../scenes/campfire.md), [The Carnival Party](../scenes/carnival.md), and [The Beach Finale](../scenes/beach-finale.md). Those pages retain the NRCU scene narratives while noting that their exact source images are unavailable.
