# ASK HERMES

> **Catalogue status:** WIP scene record derived from the 123mikeyd NRCU Vault.

## Recorded scene

The NRCU description places [Turbo Fit](../characters/turbo-fit.md) and [Teknium](../characters/teknium.md) back-to-back before a large glowing panel marked **"ASK HERMES"** in a cyberpunk city. Its golden-white light is compared with a digital billboard, server bank or portal.

### Characters recorded in the scene

- [Turbo Fit](../characters/turbo-fit.md), on the left, has shoulder-length wavy dark-brown hair and a scruffy beard. He holds a hand near his mouth in contemplation and wears an open red-and-black plaid flannel, ripped jeans, combat boots and a silver waist chain. The source contrasts his bohemian rock-and-roll styling with the technological setting.
- [Teknium](../characters/teknium.md), on the right, points toward the panel. The legacy text gives him spiky bright-green hair, a red mask or bandana over his lower face, a dark-green tactical jacket with a sergeant chevron, strapped black cargo trousers and boots with green light strips.

The red face covering is specific to this legacy scene description and conflicts with Teknium's current profile, which specifies connected triangular shades and no mask. The unavailable source image prevents that discrepancy from being resolved, so both records remain explicitly labelled.

## Questions around the panel

The source frames the surrounding speech bubbles as questions from the public:

- "WHAT'S THE TRUTH?"
- "HOW DOES IT WORK?"
- "WHAT'S NEXT?"
- "WHY?"
- "HOW?"
- "WHERE DO WE START?"
- "WHAT'S THE ANSWER?"
- "WHO KNOWS?"
- "WHAT IF?"

## Background and composition

A grid of translucent frames forms a memory wall behind the pair. The small frames are described as showing the two characters with crowds, in urban settings and in mysterious poses, creating a hall-of-mirrors or data-stream effect. Faint constellation or neural-network lines connect the corners. Pedestrian silhouettes along the bottom place the scene in a public street.

## Recorded style

The legacy page describes anime and cyberpunk artwork: cool blue ambient light against the panel's warm glow, with neon, data streams, military technology and a gritty city atmosphere. The layered memory wall supplies depth.

## NRCU interpretation

The source reads the scene as a search for truth. Turbo Fit represents chaos and art; Teknium represents order and strategy. Together they consult Hermes as an AI, oracle or search engine. The speech bubbles feed public questions into that system, while the memory wall suggests that each question and answer joins a continuing record.

The NRCU text calls Hermes "both the messenger and the message": a bridge between human curiosity and cosmic knowledge. This is retained as source interpretation, not added catalogue canon.
