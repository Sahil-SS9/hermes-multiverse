## Confirmed facts

- Sahil is the owner and curator of the Hermes Multiverse archive; this character entry is the archive owner's self-insert representation.
- The 2026-09-01 reference audit lists Sahil among established identities with a six-sheet style set.
- Sahil's nine style exemplar sheets served as the **style references for the whole library** — the style matrix (anime, chibi, claymation/stop-motion, Disney, gothic, hyper-realistic, ink-pen sketch, mecha, Rick and Morty) was exemplified by Sahil's sheets before being applied to other characters.
- The NRCU readme credits Sahil among the makers of the **Hermes SR72** arts-dept compilation video ("made with Hermes Agent, Arts Bro, Sahil, and amglolification", [watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/wNITra1sT-M)).
