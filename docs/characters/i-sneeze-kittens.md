## Documented visual record

The supplied source sheet depicts I Sneeze Kittens with a small black-and-cream dog labelled **Moxie**. The human figure has angular golden-blond hair, bright-green sunglasses, a red suit with mustard-yellow bands, a black diagonal strap, pale gloves, a blue wrist device, and black shoes. The sheet also shows a silver-grey chain. Moxie has long black ears, amber-brown eyes, a black collar, a dark back patch, and a curved fluffy tail.

Those details describe the sheet. They do not establish a real person's appearance or an official Nous Research role.
