## Documented record

The legacy NRCU record identifies quark2world at about **02:09** in the fan-made video [*Thank You Nous Research*](../music-videos/thank-you-nous-research-arts-bro.md), operating a large industrial machine in a workshop. Its visible notes describe a bald, pointed-eared humanoid figure in a pale work coat beside copper-toned fittings, a hand wheel, and indicator lights.

The same legacy page says official Hermes documentation credited `@quark2world` with custom skins for the HermelinChat GUI. The public [hermelinChat repository](https://github.com/quarker1337/hermelinChat) is retained as a related project citation. The [amglolification channel](https://www.youtube.com/@amglolification) hosts related HermelinChat and Tekpunk material, including the catalogued [*Tekpunk: One More Prompt*](../music-videos/tekpunk-one-more-prompt.md), but the available archive does not establish that quark2world owns or operates that channel.
