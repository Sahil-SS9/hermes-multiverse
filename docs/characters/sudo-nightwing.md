## Fan-created character record

The NRCU design presents a systems ally and rapid-response defender with dense dark-brown curls, an angular blue eye mask, a dark-brown moustache, and a ninjato sword. Its Citadel-defence sequence and combat role are fan lore. The design is an inspired counterpart to a blue-masked comic archetype, not DC's Dick Grayson or Robin, and no ownership of those third-party characters is implied.
