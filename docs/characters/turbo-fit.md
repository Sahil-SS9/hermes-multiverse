## Catalogue design

The source sheet depicts Turbo Fit with long, messy dark reddish-brown hair, dark sunglasses, punk or grunge clothing, boots, metal and leather accessories, and a bass guitar. An `NR` mark appears on the shirt in the legacy description.

## NRCU character lore

The legacy sheet casts Turbo Fit as an open-source rebel, fighter, builder, hardware specialist, and local-model enthusiast. It lists a bass guitar as the signature item and "NVIDIA Blackwell 6000" as a weapon-like prop. These are fan-created character labels and symbols, not a verified personal biography, hardware inventory, or official Nous Research role.

The video log for [*Thank You Nous Research*](../music-videos/thank-you-nous-research-arts-bro.md) identifies Sovthpaw / Turbo Fit at about **02:40** in a small home workspace with a tent-like hut and chalkboard. Mike's description of an unfazed effort to keep track of everything is retained as source commentary.

Turbo Fit also appears in the archived descriptions of [ASK HERMES](../scenes/ask-hermes.md), [The Campfire](../scenes/campfire.md), [The Carnival Party](../scenes/carnival.md), and [The Beach Finale](../scenes/beach-finale.md).
