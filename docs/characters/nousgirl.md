## Confirmed identity

The updated NRCU record says that **Nous Girl** means the girl represented in the Nous Research logo and explicitly states that she is not a real person. The archive owner selected a neutral grayscale portrait as the current upstream character reference.

The video record for [*Thank You Nous Research*](../music-videos/thank-you-nous-research-arts-bro.md) places NousGirl around **02:00**, receiving a lantern from an unidentified long-haired figure. [ee.dd](ee-dd.md) appears as the small green frog-like meme avatar on her shoulder.

## Current canonical identity sheet

The existing canonical sheet remains the active production reference. It is a production-ready multi-view sheet with front, side and back body coverage, expression studies, accessory breakdowns, short dark wavy hair, over-ear headphones, a black dress and tall black boots.

It is retained because the newly selected upstream portrait is a low-resolution, single head-and-shoulders sketch. A one-view portrait cannot safely replace an approved turnaround without a deliberate identity decision.

The selected portrait shows a youthful anime-style face, large eyes, layered hair with bangs and a small headband detail. It does not show headphones or a full outfit. The supplementary studies establish a dark bob, headband, large over-ear headphones, collared clothing and several expressions; one panel adds cyan eyes and blue effects. These studies support some anchors in the current sheet but do not prove that every outfit, hair shape or powered state is canonical.

The action-scene PNG is a higher-fidelity upstream encoding of artwork already preserved as an earlier JPEG source-evidence variant. Both are retained with a documented near-duplicate relationship rather than counted as two distinct appearances.

## Reconciliation decision still needed

A human review must decide:

1. whether the neutral logo portrait, the headphone studies, or the existing multi-view sheet is the primary identity controller;
2. which hair shape and headband details are canonical;
3. whether the black dress or collared/headphone study outfit is standard, or whether they are named variants;
4. whether the cyan-eye/blue-effect panel is a real state or exploratory art;
5. whether the existing nine style sheets remain approved against the reconciled identity.

Until then, the current style sheets remain catalogued, but the design record is explicitly marked `upstream-reference-reconciliation-pending`.
