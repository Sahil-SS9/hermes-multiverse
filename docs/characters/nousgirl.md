## Confirmed identity

The updated NRCU record says that **Nous Girl** means the girl represented in the Nous Research logo and explicitly states that she is not a real person. The archive owner selected a neutral grayscale portrait as an upstream character reference.

The video record for [*Thank You Nous Research*](../music-videos/thank-you-nous-research-arts-bro.md) places NousGirl around **02:00**, receiving a lantern from an unidentified long-haired figure. [ee.dd](ee-dd.md) appears as the small green frog-like meme avatar on her shoulder.

## Canonical identity decision

Sahil confirmed on 2026-09-08 that the existing canonical multi-view sheet remains the identity controller. It provides front, side and back body coverage, expressions, accessory breakdowns, short dark wavy hair, a black dress, tall black boots and the iconic large over-ear headphones.

The headphones are mandatory whenever NousGirl is depicted. A direct review of all nine current style sheets found them present in every character panel, with one consistent character across each sheet. Result: **9 PASS, 0 FAIL; no regeneration required**.
