## Documented source

The public [*GGB Introduction* video](https://youtu.be/YSkovtkyOmg) supplies the preserved visual and story record. The local portrait is an uncropped frame retained from that source.

Visible anchors include a small translucent green gummy body, clear wings, curled antennae, a single yellow stripe, goo at the mouth, and a yellow-and-black three-sided emblem on the back. The catalogue does not decide whether the emblem is a warning or radioactive symbol.

## Creator-supplied fiction

Within the introduction's fictional presentation, GGB is a magical gummy bee whose goo promotes botanical growth. A drop of golden honey wakes GGB beside a riverbank, and nearby flowers respond to the goo. These are story claims from the source video, not biological facts, personal biography, or official Nous Research canon.
